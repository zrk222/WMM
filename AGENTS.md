# WMM contributor boundaries

Keep the application loopback-only. Never commit operational databases, credentials, real customer app profiles or private research. Tests use synthetic data and isolated temporary databases. Run regression and distribution/privacy checks before release.

External effects require exact current authority. The owner may approve a bounded supported campaign scope for agent publishing and paid Meta effects, with account, currency, content, rights, targeting, expiry, quotas and media-liability ceilings. Default spending remains zero. Agents cannot create or widen owner authority. Provider credentials alone do not authorize effects. Preserve App Store maker/checker controls and every effect-specific gate.

Queued, prepared, rendered, provider-verified delivery and measured app outcomes are distinct. Preserve durable IDs/receipts and unresolved budget holds; never replay an uncertain mutation. OFF and pause need actual provider readback, and do not undo incurred charges. No growth, sales or virality guarantee.

Native iOS remains an independent on-device product requirement; do not substitute a desktop companion or claim physical-device acceptance from simulator evidence.

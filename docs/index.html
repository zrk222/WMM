A campaign journey you can follow.

01 / SET YOUR GOAL

Start with what matters.

Describe the app, its audience and the change you want to test. Bring research, app proof, clips and supported account connections.

02 / WATCH THE WORK

Give your agent room to work.

Approve a bounded job or automation scope. Follow structured tasks and inspect the creative. Handle exceptions that need a new decision.

03 / REVIEW RESULTS

Leave with a next move.

Open the files, inspect receipts and compare available measurements. Export a PDF or data report. Choose what to continue, revise or stop.

Autonomy with a clear agreement

Your consent sets the boundaries.

Approve one action or a finite scope for supported recurring work. The agreement makes the important choices visible before activation.

  • Agent, app and connected accounts
  • Actions, content and destinations
  • Schedule, expiry and call quotas
  • Budget ceilings and stop rules

Agents operate inside current authority. Future work stops when authority is revoked; completed external actions still have their own receipts and recovery paths.

One workspace for the moving parts

From useful intelligence to a useful decision.

Research worth acting on

App context, source snapshots, evidence cards, saved research and title/thumbnail ideas. Viral-only discovery requires 100,000 views and 10× a documented creator baseline. UGC has a separate intake path.

Creative you can shape

Copy packs, local or selected Drive media, UGC, trimming, ordering and stitching. Hypit guided production, preflight and verified-render reuse; richer projects use the editor and Studio.

Supported campaign execution

Facebook Page posts and fixed Meta engagement campaigns with exact owner scope, reviewed content and rights, limits, scheduling, retained IDs, reconciliation and protective pause.

Analytics with a source

Supported Apple, Google Play, PostHog, Clarity and RevenueCat paths plus imports. Reports retain dates, definitions, denominators and missing data. PDF and structured exports keep the evidence shareable.

A better app sales journey

ASO, ASA and AEO worksheets, store checks, funnels, paywall and subscription experiments, cohorts and tracking plans. App Store promotional text follows a separate maker/checker approval path.

People and agents use the same workspace

Personal-agent CLI/MCP, scoped grants, durable jobs, visible progress and owner reports. Profiles cover Grok Bot, Instinct, Hark Pro, Meta Muse, Codex/Dots, Claude Duet and generic agent teams.

Current product: local workspaces with supported automation and partial analytics coverage. Shared cloud team dashboards, broader campaign publishing and complete KPI coverage are in development. Inspect the capability map.

The outcome to work toward

More time building. Better marketing decisions.

Use WMM to reduce repeated research, shorten creative cycles and make informed decisions about installs, activation, conversion and retention. Receipts prove operations; connected measurements show what changed. The dogfooding trial has not yet established growth or time savings.

Setup, privacy and current availability

Windows and Ubuntu preview installers include the runtime. Desktop protected credentials use Windows Credential Manager or configured Linux Secret Service; the macOS adapter is unsupported. Native iPhone/iPad acceptance is separate. Keep the desktop server private on loopback; choose an explicitly configured owner-managed host for always-on operation.

Personal and internal business use is free under the repository license. Optional providers have their own costs. Resale, hosting and paid client services require a commercial agreement. Accounts stay private; default ad spending is zero.

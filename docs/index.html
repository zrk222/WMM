The tools behind the work

App context and research

App workspaces, twelve-stage pipeline, research collections, source history and next actions. Viral-only: 100,000 views AND 10× documented creator baseline. UGC intake is separate.

Packaging and production

Title/thumbnail research, remix/feed previews, taste guidance and copy packs. Upload, order, trim and stitch clips. Hypit: timed text, preflight and verified-render reuse. Editor/Studio: richer projects.

Store and sales journey

ASO, ASA and AEO; funnel, paywall and subscription experiments; cohorts and event/billing imports. App Store promotional text has separate maker/checker approval.

Analytics and evidence

Apple, Google Play, PostHog, Clarity and RevenueCat paths plus imports. Dated observations, KPI definitions, missing data, reports and workspace snapshots.

Approved campaign execution

Facebook Page posts and fixed Meta engagement campaigns: exact owner scope, reviewed content/rights/targeting, expiry, quotas, media ceilings, saved IDs, reconciliation and pause. Other networks retain execution limits.

Agent and human control

79 CLI/MCP operations, scoped grants, bounded automation, approvals, progress, durable jobs and recovery. Token/cache observations stay separate from estimated billing.

Setup, privacy and current limits

Python 3.11+ powers the core workspace; optional providers/video tools need setup. Protected desktop credentials: Windows and configured Linux/Ubuntu, not macOS. Native iPhone/iPad acceptance is separate. Keep the desktop server private.

Use your own private accounts. Default spend is zero; campaigns require exact authority. Media limits exclude taxes, fees and exchange effects. Live outcomes need account evidence. Personal/internal use is free; resale, hosting and paid client services need a commercial agreement.

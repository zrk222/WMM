From reference to reviewed video

See how your video takes shape.

A synthetic process map for the walkthrough below. This page connects no accounts and fetches no footage. It runs no model, renders no video and publishes no posts.

  1. 01 / Research the angle

    Qualify a viral reference only with ≥100,000 views AND 10× a documented creator baseline. Keep the source and evidence; missing baseline means unqualified.

  2. 02 / Capture with permission

    Use your own app footage or material with owner-approved reuse rights. Saving a reference or collection does not grant reuse permission.

  3. 03 / Revise in Hypit

    Shape the hook, stitch approved clips, revise captions and choose the channel format. Review an editable preview before the final output.

  4. 04 / Check taste and app proof

    Apply strict taste review: readable captions, a clear hook and authentic app proof. Fix failed checks before requesting approval.

  5. 05 / Approve the exact creative

    Review the exact version and render scope. A render receipt records the output; it does not establish publication or marketing results.

  6. 06 / Publish, then monitor

    Exact owner approval covers the destination and publishing scope. Inspect delivery receipts and connected measurements; unknown results stay unknown.

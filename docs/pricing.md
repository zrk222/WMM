# Pricing and commercial use

This page answers two questions: **can I use WMM for free?** and **how do I buy a
commercial license?**

## Free — no payment

Free for personal evaluation, education, and your organization's internal
business use. That includes marketing apps, products and services your
organization owns or operates, with employees and contractors working on your
behalf. You can modify it privately and you do not have to publish changes.

Internal use does **not** include running WMM as a public hosted service for
other organizations, reselling it, white-labeling it, or using it to deliver paid
marketing or automation services to outside clients.

## Commercial — written agreement required

**Agency pilot: US$900 per year for one agency.** This is the starting offer for
using WMM privately to deliver marketing services to outside clients. Confirm
the covered operators, client work, and any applicable taxes in writing before
payment. Public hosting, redistribution and white-label use are quoted separately.

A separate paid written agreement is required before you:

- sell, rent, sublicense or commercially redistribute WMM or a derivative;
- distribute a repackaged or white-label product;
- provide hosted, SaaS, API, managed-agent or other third-party access;
- use WMM to deliver marketing, content, campaign or automation services to
  outside clients as part of a paid service.

Renaming, bundling, private modifications or charging indirectly do not avoid
this. Publishing your changes does not replace permission or payment.

## How to request a license

Contact the repository owner through the published profile or repository contact
channel for [zrk222/WMM](https://github.com/zrk222/WMM). Say:

- who will use it and how many people;
- whether it will be self-hosted, hosted for others, or client-facing;
- what services or distribution you plan;
- the term you need.

**Do not post credentials, customer data or confidential terms in a public
issue.** Use a private channel for contract details. If you are unsure whether
your use is free, ask before you host for others or charge for the work.

## What the agreement covers

Parties, permitted services and distribution, duration, fees or royalties,
payment terms and any reporting obligations. Fees are negotiated in writing; the
license sets no automatic royalty and silence is not permission.

## Current status

There is no automatic checkout. The pilot price is an offer to discuss a
written agreement; paying on its own does not grant permission. Free personal
and internal use remains available under the existing license.

## What is not included

Provider charges (Meta, Apple, analytics and video tools) are separate. No growth,
sales, revenue or time-saving outcome is guaranteed. Nothing here is legal advice;
have qualified counsel review terms before a commercial sale.

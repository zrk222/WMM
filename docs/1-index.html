Owner approval, in clear terms

One agreement. Clear boundaries.

Review the agent, app, accounts, exact content and destinations, schedule, expiry, quotas, budget and stop rules together. The agreement summarizes existing permissions; it never creates broader authority.

  • Use an editable research, organic or bounded Meta profile
  • Approve source reuse, exact publication and paid scope separately
  • Set and explicitly extend deadlines; pause or revoke access
  • Inspect quotas, budget, stop rules and receipts when needed

A grant has its own expiry and revocation. Provider posts, account changes and charges already made keep separate status and recovery paths.

About WMM

Give every app a clearer next marketing move.

Built for founders and marketing teams promoting one app or a portfolio. On Day 1, turn one question into a first evidence packet. Around Day 30, with regular use, your dated research, approved work and available results can help you focus effort and make better-informed decisions.

The potential benefit is less repeated work and fewer unsupported bets. Receipts prove operations; connected measurements show what changed. Growth and time savings are not guaranteed, and the dogfooding trial has not yet established either.

Setup, privacy and current availability

Windows and Ubuntu preview installers include the runtime; Windows is unsigned. Desktop protected credentials use Windows Credential Manager or configured Linux Secret Service; the macOS adapter is unsupported. Native iPhone/iPad acceptance is separate. Keep the desktop server private on loopback; optionally configure an owner-managed Linux host for approved scheduled work. An offline host cannot execute jobs.

Personal and internal business use is free under the repository license. Optional providers have their own costs. Resale, hosting and paid client services require a commercial agreement. Accounts stay private; default ad spending is zero.

# WMM licensing

## Plain answers

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Can I use WMM for my own app or my company's internal work? | Yes, free. |
| Can I change it and keep the changes private? | Yes. |
| Do my outputs and customer data belong to me? | Yes. |
| Can I host it for other organizations, resell it or white-label it? | No, not without a paid written commercial agreement. |
| Can I use it to deliver paid marketing or automation services to clients? | No, not without a paid written commercial agreement. |
| Is this OSI open source? | No. It is source-available. Prior Apache 2.0 grants are preserved. |
| Is there a price list? | Internal use is free. Commercial use is quoted in writing; see [pricing](pricing.md). |

WMM is source-available. The [current license](../LICENSE) permits free personal evaluation and internal business use, including marketing your own apps. Your outputs and customer data remain yours. Private modifications do not require publication.

Resale, repackaging, white-label distribution, hosting, SaaS/API access and paid marketing or automation services for outside clients require a separate paid written commercial agreement from an authorized rights holder. Publishing modifications does not substitute for payment or permission. Fees are negotiated; no default royalty is imposed.

The repository owner administers requests through [zrk222/WMM](https://github.com/zrk222/WMM). Do not share private customer information or credentials in issues. This repository does not yet provide a checkout or issue a commercial license automatically.

## Prior rights and component boundaries

The change applies prospectively to newly licensed material and new licensor-owned modifications, beginning with the first revision containing this notice dated 2026-11-02. Existing Apache 2.0 grants are not withdrawn. The previous license is preserved [verbatim](../licenses/Apache-2.0.txt); previously published code can still be used under those terms. A LICENSE replacement cannot make old Apache code exclusive or compel its existing recipients to pay. Third-party libraries, media and separately licensed components retain their licenses.

Before issuing commercial licenses, verify ownership and commercial-relicensing permissions for included contributions and dependencies. Repository administration alone does not establish all copyrights. New contributors keep copyright; obtain separate rights where commercial sublicensing requires them. This change is a software permission policy, not a technical paywall or a completed legal ownership audit. Have qualified counsel review the terms and intended sales agreement before selling licenses.

For a commercial license, acquisition, or change of license, use the
[IP diligence checklist](IP-DILIGENCE.md). It records the actual Git boundary
and the evidence required to clear material for the proposed transaction.

The Apache grant is perpetual, royalty-free and irrevocable subject to its terms: [Apache 2.0 sections 2–4](https://apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0.html). Commercial restrictions mean the new terms are [source-available rather than OSI open source](https://opensource.org/osd).

# Try WMM on your first run

Own or market an app? Help us learn whether you can use WMM to prepare one useful marketing experiment in 45 minutes.

[Check your eligibility](https://github.com/zrk222/WMM/issues/new?template=founder-trial.yml)

You can take part in the outside-founder trial if you own or market an app and have never used WMM or seen its demo. The project owner, contributors, and demo viewers are excluded. The owner will review eligibility and arrange an uncoached session. A GitHub issue is a public self-report pending review; it does not qualify you or count as a trial result.

Use the [official releases page](https://github.com/zrk222/WMM/releases) for the desktop preview agreed for your session. Windows previews are unsigned and SmartScreen may block installation. Do not bypass a warning for this trial; report where you stopped.

## The 45-minute session

1. **Consent and setup (5 min).** Use synthetic app facts or non-sensitive facts about your own app. This tests first use, and no real provider credentials are needed.
2. **Task (25 min).** Using WMM, prepare one marketing experiment you could actually review, with a clear outcome and the evidence still missing.
3. **Think aloud (5 min).** Say where you hesitated, misread something, or got stuck. The observer will record these without answering product questions during the task.
4. **Debrief (10 min).** In one sentence, say what WMM does, what you would do next, and whether you would use it again for this app. Quotes are optional and only recorded with permission.

[Report your first run](https://github.com/zrk222/WMM/issues/new?template=founder-trial-result.yml), even if you could not install, stopped early, or needed help. Do not retry to replace a failed first attempt. Your issue is public and linked to your GitHub account. Leave out emails, credentials, customer data, private analytics or exports, and attachments containing private information. Use a generic app category instead of an app or customer name.

The [maintainer protocol](VALIDATION-PROTOCOL.md) defines how first runs are reviewed. A submitted issue is not verified cohort evidence. This trial measures usability and reuse intent; it does not establish retention, revenue, or growth.

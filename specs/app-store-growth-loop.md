# App Store growth loop

Status: staged integration. The Apple Ads privacy-row repair and next action for each research opportunity are independently testable; the scheduled loop and live SUDHĪ measurements are separate acceptance gates.

WMM uses one workflow to choose, prepare, and measure the next store intervention for the selected app. The weekly report leads with one proposed change, its reason, and what success would look like. The app, store identifier, locale, territory, release, source hashes, threshold, and review date stay attached to that decision. Missing measurements must direct a repair before the system recommends spending or claims a result.

## Acquisition signals and actions

| Signal | Decision | Guard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Low discoverability, satisfactory conversion, and a supported search intent | Review one app name, subtitle, or keyword-set change in an editable localization. Freeze a comparable discovery baseline first. | App Store search includes ads. Treat the segment as mixed; never subtract incompatible paid counts or label it organic rank. |
| Sufficient discovery but weak page engagement or first-time downloads | Review one first-screenshot or app-preview treatment using Product Page Optimization. | Keep listing metadata and paid conditions stable where feasible. Use Apple's treatment results rather than an assumed lift. |
| Different high-intent audiences need different proof | Draft one Custom Product Page with matching images and approved keywords for that localization. | Organic routing requires a visible, approved page and uses keywords from the approved app version. Keep each page's intent distinct. |
| Current paid query evidence and an approved budget and loss limit | Review one capped exact-match Apple Ads test, or a separately capped Search Match discovery test. | Paid terms, search popularity, and paid impression-share rank remain separate from organic position and exact search volume. Views and redownloads do not substitute for first-time acquisitions. |
| Visibility data missing, late, or incomparable | Repair the collector or collect a mature baseline, then revisit the same decision. | Missing or suppressed values are unknown, not zero. Do not infer ranking, purchases, or causality. |

Query research preserves the observed source separately from the analyst's inferred intent. Long-tail questions guide the page promise, creative, and precise paid tests; they are not copied wholesale into the keyword field. The keyword proposal uses the App Store Connect limit of 100 UTF-8 bytes, comma-separated terms, no repeated title/subtitle terms, and no competitor brands. Promotional text is for conversion messaging, not indexing. Maintain a separate, explicit storefront and exact-query observation for any claimed organic position; “not found within checked positions” has a different meaning from an observation that was never made.

## Integration and verification

1. Reuse current Apple discovery/download receipts and the read-only Apple Ads search term and keyword reports. Preserve provider-declared pagination scope, hidden query rows and currency/timezone. Define comparable windows before reading results; keep conversions and first-time downloads separate from ad installs. Add paid impression share as a separate, evidence-bound observation when the read path is implemented and verified.
2. Reuse `MARKET_RESEARCH`, `ASO_LISTING`, saved benchmark comparisons, and the existing weekly exception inbox. Join only current, immutable app and storefront evidence. Place one intervention and the missing prerequisites in the inbox. Show why it was selected and the alternative the current evidence does not justify. High volume never overrides product fit.
3. Stage exact before/after drafts and source-bound approval through existing authority. No keyword, listing, CPP, Product Page Optimization, bid, or budget mutation is implied by a read or draft. Keep a separate stop rule, restoration version, and evidence receipt.
4. After a fixed observation window, record continue, revise, stop, or collect, including unsuccessful and inconclusive tests. Compare the same source, market, locale, denominator, date policy and attribution frame. For a paid pilot, show spend, taps, first-time downloads, and cost per first-time download when defined. Report CAC or D7/D30 ROAS only from a joined, mature acquisition and revenue cohort with compatible scope and currency.
5. Verify with both published SUDHĪ and WizeMe's separate workspace: no cross-app source or marketing actions, no duplicate proposal for identical evidence, no early edits to the test window, no false organic claim from paid traffic, and no automatic paid or store write. Live checks require exact connected data and authority. No uplift target is reported as achieved without observations.

Marketing acceptance requires a real, source-bound intervention followed by a comparable, mature observation window and a business decision. Freeze an owner-reviewed minimum useful change in visibility or first-time downloads, the traffic/sample floor, and a conversion guardrail before exposure. Record the exact store or campaign action, its receipt, source and exclusions, before/after windows or randomized treatment, and the next decision. A completed job, internal fixture, or unattributable change does not satisfy this acceptance.

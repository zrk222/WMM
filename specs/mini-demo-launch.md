# Shipped-change mini-demo launch

## Requirements (EARS)
- When current shipment evidence is recorded, WMM shall propose topics from the last 30 days and show old or changed-source candidates separately. Shipment evidence is owner-supplied, not independent verification.
- When preparing a mini-demo, WMM shall bind one shipped change to a current SHORT_VIDEO brief, authentic capture assets, captions, an exact channel and a registered observational campaign for that creative.
- Before mini-demo publication approval, WMM shall require an explicit audience, problem and reason to watch within the first ten seconds, bound to the exact brief hash. Changes to product proof or creative shall invalidate the gate.
- When preparing or inspecting a demo, WMM shall return stable feature, hook, render, approval/publish and receipt steps, their prerequisites and evidence. Preparation shall issue no render, spending, approval or publishing authority.
- When inspecting an actual render, WMM shall verify saved artifact bytes through the existing render path. A queued or prepared artifact shall never count as rendered or published.
- When a publication receipt is selected, WMM shall require the same owner, channel, exact rendered artifact and exact campaign link, and validate the existing publication receipt chain. A provider-read receipt does not independently establish public visibility or business lift.
- When a registered demo is used for publication, the existing approval/dispatch proof gate shall reject a changed hook, product source, channel or campaign link.
- When reporting signups, WMM shall reuse the existing source-bound tagged landing-to-registration study. A channel tag alone shall never establish conversion.
- When importing retention observations, WMM shall require the study's declared collector and same source subject namespace, exact tag, explicit consent/bot/internal-test state, bounded events, source receipt and coverage. It shall count returns during [registration + N days, registration + N + 1 days) only for linked eligible registrations.
- When coverage or cohort maturity is incomplete, WMM shall retain unknown rates rather than report zero. Same-event duplicates shall not inflate results; conflicting event identities shall fail.
- Existing owner approval, rights, destination, call limit and spending controls shall remain authoritative. New operations shall remain unbundled from standing policies.

## Acceptance
`tests.test_mini_demo` exercises real protected operations and current creative evidence. `tests.test_mini_demo_retention` checks mature source joins, duplicates, identity conflicts, unmatched subjects, consent/bot/internal exclusions, and unknown coverage. Existing publication authority, execution and report suites remain mandatory.

## Limits
This is an agent-operated local workflow using existing screen-capture assets and publication-compatible Hypit render/approval operations, not a new autonomous recorder or one-click public publisher. Live channel delivery and external signup/retention outcomes need actual authorized evidence. Independent human review pending.

# Goal: find and win overlooked store search intent

WMM helps an agent find which audiences an app can serve, prepare a matching
listing experiment, and use measured outcomes to choose the next change. The
notebook's keyword workbook is a starting workflow, not a dataset for WMM:
its recommendations describe a budgeting app and its volume estimates are not
validated for the intended app.

1. Research one app, release and storefront. Find three distinct buyer problems,
   retain dated search evidence, compare competing promises, and document what
   the current build can deliver. Separate observed search data, analyst
   hypotheses and unsupported gaps. Use existing Market Research entry and
   agent catalog. First acceptance: identical inputs produce the same review
   order and clear evidence gaps; invalid and stale evidence cannot advance a test.
2. Compare metadata alternatives under each store's field limits. Explain which
   intent coverage each option adds or gives up. Draft one change, authentic
   assets, and a matched store page when the provider permits it. Use the current
   listing validator, creative brief and approval surfaces.
3. Preregister and run an approved test with a frozen control, one primary
   conversion metric, sample and stopping rule. Monitor verified provider
   readbacks, protect activation quality where observable, and recommend keep,
   revise, stop or collect. Keep organic, paid and tagged referral evidence
   separate. Avoid inferring organic keyword revenue from page-level outcomes.

First dogfood: WizeMe, U.S. Apple App Store, organic only. Compare difficult
conversation practice, decision reflection and daily follow-through as
hypotheses. Require a current store listing/build and source receipts before
moving an intent into experiment review. Track the full funnel where attribution
supports it; leave unsupported counts unknown. No provider changes are implied by
a saved research packet. Extend to Google Play using its own listing capabilities.

## Additional research findings from all notebook sources

- Expand from several close competitors' observed query footprints. Include
  smaller apps already visible for the job, and adjacent problems their listings
  or reviews expose. Separate captured rank, suggestion presence, and provider
  estimates; compare only like-for-like provider, date, territory and scale.
- Protect proven terms: show what an alternative displaces, record a stable set
  of unchanged comparison queries, and flag concurrent launches, promotions,
  pricing and acquisition changes when reading rank or conversion movement.
- Route by the bottleneck: low visibility calls for a keyword or positioning test;
  visibility without installs calls for a message or first screenshot test;
  installs without activation call for fixing the promise or first-run path.
  Use rates with their actual denominators and show unobservable stages.
- Localize the user's problem, not just the phrase. Compare storefronts separately
  and require that screenshots and the first in-app experience serve the language.
  No fixed keyword score, density rule or cross-indexing guarantee.
- Hypit and AEO supply new language and angles. Require separate store evidence
  before counting those angles as search demand, and prefer an authentic product
  demonstration with tagged, measurable visits over a raw engagement threshold.

## Acceptance evidence for the full upgrade

| Gap | Required proof |
| --- | --- |
| Collection | One permitted storefront capture and one repeat with comparable locale, terms, positions and source health. |
| Competitive discovery | Three app-specific intent clusters, with adjacent opportunities and receipts for inclusion or exclusion. |
| Metadata allocation | At least two valid alternatives with gains, displaced coverage and a preserved control. |
| Localization | Native wording and first-use readiness verified separately for each target storefront. |
| Creative continuity | Approved authentic proof, a matching store-page draft and a tracked distribution path. |
| Reporting | Desktop and mobile results plus PDF and JSON exports of the same decisions, evidence and unknowns. |
| Experiment loop | One real preregistered test, provider readback and a decision with denominators and coverage. |
| Agent operations | Replayable agent input, scoped permissions, durable history, explicit collection failures and no unapproved mutation. |
